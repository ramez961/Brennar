GOOD TO KNOW

Before you head out.

Is this a live store?

This is a working Brennar storefront demo. Products, photographs, specifications, and prices are illustrative concepts. Checkout places a real order with your name, phone number, and delivery address; no online payment is taken.

How do sizes work?

Apparel offers demo sizes S–XXL. Packs and bottles have capacity options. The product dialog shows the options available for each concept item. Final measurements will be added when real suppliers are selected.

Shipping, returns & product protection

Shipping regions, delivery fees, return terms, and certified protection ratings are not yet established. These will be supplied before real sales begin. No safety certification is claimed for these concept products.
